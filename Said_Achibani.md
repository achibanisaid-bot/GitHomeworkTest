My name is Said Achibani, and I’m happy to introduce myself!

🏊 My Favourite Sport

My favourite sport is swimming. I have loved swimming since I was a child because it is a very healthy and complete sport. It helps me stay fit, strengthens my muscles, and works almost every part of the body. For me, swimming is not just a sport; it is also a great way to relax and feel good.

🍲 My Favourite Food

My favourite food is traditional couscous made by my mum. I love it because it combines different vegetables with delicious meat, creating a wonderful mix of flavours. It is absolutely delicious, especially when my mum prepares it. For me, it is more than just food because it reminds me of my family, our traditions, and the importance of sharing meals together.

🎵 My Favourite Music

One of my favourite songs is Everyday Life by Coldplay.

You can listen to it here: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HmiFxqdgq4

I like this song because it delivers a powerful message about humanity. The song reminds me that we should choose peace, understanding, and kindness instead of hatred and division.

🌍 An Interesting Fact About Me

An interesting fact about me is that I would love to travel around the world and discover different cultures, meet people from different backgrounds, and learn about their ways of life. I believe that travelling can change the way we see the world and help us understand that, even though we may speak different languages and have different traditions, we are all part of the same human family.
